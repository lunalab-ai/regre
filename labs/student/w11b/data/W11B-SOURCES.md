# W11B data provenance

The author offers the companion datasets for download at https://www1.aucegypt.edu/faculty/hadi/RABE6/ . These teaching CSVs contain only the corresponding numerical companion data, with unchanged values/order and an added source_row. This is attribution, not a claim that the book or its solutions are openly licensed.

- ski-sales-w11b.csv: Ski.Sales.txt, 40 quarters 1964Q1–1973Q4, Date/Sales/PDI. Sales and PDI retain original scales; no unverified physical unit is added.
- education-w11b.csv: Edu.Expend.txt, 150 observations, 50 states × 1960/1970/1975. Y per-capita public education expenditure; X1 personal income; X2 under-18 population per 1000; X3 urban population per 1000. Region metadata are retained. Baseline year1975.
- fertilizer-w11b.csv: Fertilizer.txt, 40 plots, four groups of10; group4 is the no-fertilizer control. For transfer practice6.9, not a precomputed answer table.
- categorical-trace-w11b.R and indicator-applications-w11b.R are byte-identical copies of the public src definitions, containing general methods and input checks, not textbook-answer lookups.

Raw scans, private solutions and other private source files are excluded. New nine-value worked examples are author-created and identified as such.
